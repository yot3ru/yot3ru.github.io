Straight terms

What you agree to, in plain words

Every project runs on the same terms. You get them in writing before you pay anything.

  • 50% to start, 50% on delivery

    No hourly billing, no metered surprises. The price we quote is the price you pay.

  • Two revision rounds, included

    Scheduled into the timeline. Extra rounds are quoted before they start, never billed after.

  • Dates agreed in writing

    If we miss a date, you hear it from us first, with a new date and the reason.

  • You own the work

    Full ownership and source files transfer to you on final payment. Nothing is held hostage.
