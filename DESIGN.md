# LUFI dark edition

LUFI uses an Ink-first editorial academia system. Bone functions as reading colour, Cinnabar marks decisions and active states, and liquid metal supplies motion without changing the brand palette.

The page behaves like a sequence of dark publication spreads. Thin Bone rules, generous margins, numbered folios, asymmetric columns and Funnel Display headings provide structure. DM Sans carries body copy and interface labels. Zodiak appears once as the pull quote.

The hero and closing section contain the same dark liquid-metal material. A wide alpha mask dissolves the shader into the Ink field so no compositing boundary crosses the text. Motion remains slow, weighted and locally responsive. Pointer deformation is calculated continuously in shader space, avoiding texture-grid artifacts. Desktop rendering targets 60 fps at up to 1.5× render scale, with a static fallback and reduced-motion support.

Sections alternate between Ink, deep Ink and a slightly raised warm-black stock. These changes separate chapters without introducing new colours. Portfolio artwork remains the brightest content after the call to action.

The navigation rail behaves like a black book spine on desktop and a compact footer index on narrow screens. Bone labels, Cinnabar active marks and 44px targets preserve clarity and touch access.

Portfolio imagery is self-initiated and follows the same three-colour world: tactile print matter, one-light portraiture, cinematic installation work, experimental title frames and editorial interface studies. A Cinnabar circle replaces the native cursor on fine pointers, easing behind movement and changing scale over interactive targets; reduced-motion and coarse-pointer users retain the native cursor.
